## What does "konstellarium" mean?

It's a [portmoneau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of the words "konstelacja" (Polish for ["constellation"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constellation), meaning a cluster of stars) and ["vivarium"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivarium), meaning an enclosed space for keeping plants or animals for observation and/or research.

It started as the name of my fantasy worldbuilding project, where the ancestral humans worship the deity of stars and cosmos and, thus call themselves Starchildren, or simply Stars. Thus, the planet they were created on and live on is a konstellarium, an anclosure for Stars where their deity observes them.

Eventually, I began using the term to refer to the concept of "all of my [blorbos](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/blorbo-from-my-shows)" and thus, that's what this site is named.

## Are these all your characters?

Most of the characters archived here are mine and I made them, yes. But some characters were made by my friends, and are simply described here to provide context for my own OCs and their stories. Those are clearly marked, though, and usually don't contain as much information as my own creations. 

These are also not all of my characters! They're just the one that hold a special place in my heart, AKA return to my daydreams and stories regularly. Some characters I made but no longer revisit, and those usually end up as free-to-use characters archived at [Aque's pond](https://www.aque.fyi/resources/characters/).

I've also made some characters, usually for RPG campaigns, that simply never started sparking enough joy to be immortalized with a detailed character sheet, and thus ended up nowhere.
